# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A single developer using their own hardware. Uses the tool at their desk or on their phone, always connected to their own inference infrastructure over a private network. Expert. Not trying to be impressed — trying to get work done without friction.

## Product Purpose

Fornax is a personal LLM workstation and thin client. Named after the Fornax constellation (the Chemical Furnace, designated by Lacaille in the 1750s). It connects to local and remote inference servers over a private network and provides a fast, distraction-free interface for chat, metrics, and artifacts. No telemetry, no accounts, no cloud. The tool exists entirely in service of the operator.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Austere. Technical. The voice of a scientific instrument, not a product.

## Anti-references

- Anything that looks like an AI startup (gradients, glowing brains, orbital rings, neural net visualizations)
- SaaS landing page aesthetics — hero sections, testimonials, pricing tiers
- "Dark mode for cool factor" — darkness earns its place by reducing distraction at night, not as branding
- Friendly-productivity tools (Notion, Linear clones)

## Design Principles

1. **Reduction is precision** — every element that can be removed should be. What remains is not sparse, it is exact.
2. **The tool disappears** — design serves the task. If the interface is noticeable, it has failed.
3. **Honest materials** — use what the platform provides. No decoration for its own sake.
4. **Named after a furnace** — warmth is latent, not absent. The furnace heats without showing flames.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single user, no specific requirements stated. Maintain legible contrast ratios throughout.
